About

I'm a student interested in business development, consulting and product: the roles where you have to understand what a customer values, make a call with imperfect data, and explain it so other people act on it.

My experience so far spans business development at Alibaba Cloud and onboarding growth work at Iris AI: one about framing value in the customer's terms, the other about getting new users to stick, and both about reading the numbers honestly.

These projects are how I build the quantitative side of that judgment: knowing when a lift is real (A/B calculator, peeking trap), what a feature is worth to customers (boba conjoint, pricing game), and how to make cost-aware calls from a model (cutoff lab).

Toolkit

  • Experiment design: power, sequential testing, false-positive control
  • Choice modeling: conjoint, logit MLE, willingness to pay
  • Decision-making under uncertainty: bandits, simulation
  • ML: leakage audits, time-based validation, cost-based cutoffs
  • Python (numpy, pandas, scikit-learn, multiprocessing), JavaScript, HTML/CSS
