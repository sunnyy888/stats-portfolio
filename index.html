About

I'm a student interested in business development, consulting and product: the roles where you have to understand what a customer values, make a call with imperfect data, and explain it so other people act on it.

My experience so far spans business development at Alibaba Cloud and onboarding growth work at Iris AI: one about framing value in the customer's terms, the other about getting new users to stick, and both about reading the numbers honestly.

These projects are how I build the quantitative side of that judgment: knowing when a lift is real (Cookie Cats readout), what customers will pay for (boba conjoint), how to make cost-aware calls from a model (overbooking optimizer), and when not to trust one (report auditor).

Toolkit

  • Experiment design: power, sample-ratio checks, sequential testing
  • Choice modeling: conjoint, logit MLE, willingness to pay, pricing
  • Decision-making under uncertainty: bandits, Monte Carlo simulation
  • ML: leakage audits, time-based validation, cost-based cutoffs, regularization
  • Python (numpy, pandas, scikit-learn, multiprocessing, Jupyter), JavaScript, HTML/CSS
Every project's code, tests and write-up is on GitHub. All pages are static and work on a phone.
